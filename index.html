OpenROC · The Language of Physical AI · Live

Open Robot Operation Commands

OpenROC (Open Robot Operation Commands) is the open protocol that connects AI intelligence to robots, actuators, and physical systems through standardized commands. Transport-agnostic, hardware-neutral, and safe by design — AI decisions on the 6COM Z1 SoC become OpenROC messages your robot executes deterministically.

Visit OpenROC
Two stacks: intent → execution
The Action Stack carries AI objectives, tasks, and trajectories — what to do. The Actuation Stack carries joint setpoints with explicit modes, limits, and timing — how. Full arms, grippers, mobile bases: one grammar.
One protocol, two encodings
OpenROC-JSON for development, debugging, and tooling. OpenROC-BIN — compact binary framing for high-rate control streams and constrained links. Semantically identical, losslessly convertible.
Safe by design
Every command carries an explicit safety class, deadline, and authority. Safety systems can inspect, admit, or reject commands before they ever reach hardware.
Adapters, not rip-and-replace
OpenROC Adapters bridge to ROS 2, CAN, CANopen, EtherCAT, Modbus, MQTT, gRPC, OPC UA, and vendor SDKs — existing robots join the protocol without changing their native stack.
